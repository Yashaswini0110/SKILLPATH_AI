# Explainability user-study protocol

Not yet run. Do not invent ratings.

## Questions (after the learner opens Why?)

1. Was the reason understandable?
2. Was it useful for deciding whether to take the resource?
3. Did the reason match the recommendation (skills, gap, scores)?

Use a 1–5 Likert scale. Collect only after consent. Do not store extra
PII. Do not ask about hiring or employment outcomes.

## What to show

The numbered facts from `explanation.facts`, not a free-form LLM story.
The optional verbalization may be shown beside the facts.

## Analysis

Report mean and median per question, plus how often question 3 is ≤ 2
(mismatch). Compare hybrid vs a baseline method on the same items.
